# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We want participation in VISTA to be respectful, harassment-free, and welcoming regardless of experience, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and constructive; give and accept review feedback gracefully.
- Assume good faith; focus on the work.
- Respect privacy: never share others' personal data or images.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome attention.
- Other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation for violations. Report concerns privately to the maintainer (`TODO: owner to add contact`).
